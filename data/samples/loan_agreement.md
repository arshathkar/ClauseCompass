RECITALS

A. The Borrower has applied for a personal loan of Rs. 5,00,000/- (Rupees Five Lakhs Only).

1. LOAN DETAILS

1.1 Loan Amount: Rs. 5,00,000/- (Rupees Five Lakhs Only)
1.2 Interest Rate: 14% per annum (reducing balance), subject to revision as per Clause 2.3
1.3 Loan Tenure: 36 months
1.4 EMI: Rs. 17,094/- per month (approximate), payable by the 5th of each month
1.5 Processing Fee: Rs. 10,000/- (2% of loan amount), deducted at disbursement

2. INTEREST AND CHARGES

2.1 Interest shall be calculated on a daily reducing balance method.

2.2 In the event of default in payment of any EMI by the due date, the Borrower shall pay penal interest at the rate of 3% per month, compounding monthly, on the overdue amount from the due date until the date of actual payment.

2.3 The Lender reserves the right to revise the interest rate at any time based on market conditions, regulatory changes, or the Borrower's credit profile. Such revised rate shall be effective from the date of the Lender's notice, and the Borrower shall be deemed to have accepted the revised rate by continuing to avail the loan facility.

2.4 The Borrower shall pay a prepayment charge of 5% of the outstanding principal for any prepayment or foreclosure of the loan.

3. SECURITY AND COLLATERAL

3.1 The loan is unsecured. The Borrower authorizes the Lender to access and share the Borrower's credit information with credit bureaus and any third parties as the Lender deems necessary.

4. LOAN RECALL AND ACCELERATION

4.1 The Lender may, at its sole discretion, recall the entire outstanding loan amount along with accrued interest, charges, and costs, by giving 7 days' notice to the Borrower, if:
  (a) The Borrower defaults on any EMI payment;
  (b) The Borrower breaches any term of this Agreement;
  (c) There is a material adverse change in the Borrower's financial condition;
  (d) The Lender deems the loan to be at risk for any reason.

5. DATA SHARING AND CONSENT

5.1 The Borrower hereby gives irrevocable consent to the Lender to collect, process, store, and share the Borrower's personal data, financial data, credit information, and transaction details with any third parties including but not limited to credit bureaus, recovery agents, group companies, affiliates, partners, service providers, and regulatory authorities.

5.2 The Borrower agrees that the Lender may contact the Borrower and the Borrower's references by phone, SMS, email, WhatsApp, or any other means for the purpose of loan servicing, recovery, or marketing of other products.

6. INDEMNIFICATION

6.1 The Borrower shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Lender, its directors, officers, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses arising from or in connection with any breach of this Agreement by the Borrower or any misrepresentation by the Borrower.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

7.2 Any dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by the Lender, in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat of arbitration shall be Mumbai, Maharashtra. The language of arbitration shall be English. The Borrower agrees that the arbitrator's decision shall be final and binding and waives any right to challenge the award.
