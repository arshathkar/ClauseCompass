We are pleased to offer you the position of Senior Software Engineer at TechNova Solutions Private Limited ("Company"), subject to the following terms and conditions:

1. POSITION AND DUTIES

1.1 You will be employed as a Senior Software Engineer, reporting to the Engineering Director.

1.2 Your primary workplace shall be the Company's office at Whitefield, Bangalore.

1.3 The Company reserves the right to transfer you to any other location, department, or role as it deems fit.

2. COMPENSATION

2.1 Your annual compensation (CTC) shall be Rs. 24,00,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Four Lakhs Only) per annum, payable monthly.

2.2 The compensation includes a variable component of Rs. 4,00,000/- (Rupees Four Lakhs Only) per annum, which shall be payable at the sole discretion of the Company based on individual and company performance. 2.3 All payments are subject to applicable tax deductions.

3. PROBATION

3.1 You shall be on probation for a period of 6 (Six) months from the date of joining.

3.2 During probation, either party may terminate this employment by giving 15 days' written notice.

3.3 Upon successful completion of probation, your employment shall be confirmed in writing.

4. NOTICE PERIOD

4.1 After confirmation, you are required to serve a notice period of 90 (Ninety) days in the event of resignation.

4.2 The Company may terminate your employment after confirmation by giving 15 days' written notice or salary in lieu thereof.

4.3 During the notice period, the Company may relieve you earlier at its discretion, in which case salary shall be paid only up to the date of relief.

5. SERVICE BOND

5.1 You agree to serve the Company for a minimum period of 2 (Two) years from the date of joining.

5.2 In the event you resign or your employment is terminated for cause before completing the minimum service period, you shall be liable to pay the Company a sum of Rs. 3,00,000/- (Rupees Three Lakhs Only) as recovery towards training and onboarding costs.

5.3 The Company shall be entitled to deduct the recovery amount from any dues payable to you including but not limited to salary, bonus, gratuity, and reimbursements.

6.2 You hereby irrevocably assign to the Company all rights, title, and interest in any Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein, without any additional compensation.

6.3 You shall execute all documents and take all actions necessary to perfect the Company's ownership of the Work Product.

7.1 During your employment and for a period of 12 (Twelve) months following the termination of your employment for any reason, you shall not directly or indirectly engage in, be employed by, or provide services to any business that competes with the Company within India.

7.2 For the same period, you shall not solicit or attempt to solicit any employee, contractor, or client of the Company.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 You shall maintain strict confidentiality regarding all proprietary information, trade secrets, business strategies, client information, and any other confidential information of the Company during and after your employment.

8.2 This obligation shall survive the termination of your employment.
