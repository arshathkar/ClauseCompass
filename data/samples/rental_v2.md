# LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT (REVISED)

[[page:1]]

This Leave and Licence Agreement ("Agreement") is made and executed on this 15th day of September, 2026.

BETWEEN

Mr. Rajesh Kumar Sharma, son of Mr. Vinod Sharma, residing at 42, MG Road, Bangalore, Karnataka 560001 (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensor");

AND

Ms. Meera Nair, daughter of Mr. Gopalan Nair, residing at 15, Anna Salai, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600002 (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensee").

WHEREAS the Licensor is the absolute owner of the residential flat bearing No. 301, Block A, Sunrise Apartments, HSR Layout, Bangalore, Karnataka 560102 ("Premises").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AND THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 "Agreement" means this Leave and Licence Agreement including all schedules and annexures.

1.2 "Licence Fee" means the monthly amount payable by the Licensee to the Licensor as specified in Clause 2.1.

1.3 "Premises" means the residential flat bearing No. 301, Block A, Sunrise Apartments, HSR Layout, Bangalore, Karnataka 560102.

1.4 "Security Deposit" means the amount deposited by the Licensee with the Licensor as specified in Clause 5.

[[page:2]]

2. LICENCE FEE

2.1 The Licensee shall pay to the Licensor a monthly licence fee of Rs. 27,500/- (Rupees Twenty-Seven Thousand Five Hundred Only) payable on or before the 5th day of each calendar month.

2.2 In case of delay in payment of the Licence Fee beyond 7 days from the due date, the Licensee shall be liable to pay penal interest at the rate of 2% per month on the outstanding amount.

3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 This Agreement shall be valid for a period of 11 (Eleven) months commencing from 1st October, 2026, and ending on 31st August, 2027.

3.2 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of 11 months each, with a 5% escalation in the Licence Fee for each renewal period, unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the expiry.

3.3 The Licensee acknowledges and agrees that the first 12 months of the Agreement shall constitute a lock-in period during which the Licensee shall not be entitled to terminate this Agreement.

3.4 In the event the Licensee terminates this Agreement during the lock-in period, the Licensee shall be liable to pay a penalty equivalent to 3 (Three) months' Licence Fee, and the Security Deposit shall be forfeited in its entirety.

[[page:3]]

4. TERMINATION

4.1 The Licensor may terminate this Agreement at any time without assigning any reason by giving 30 days' written notice to the Licensee.

4.2 The Licensee may terminate this Agreement after the lock-in period by giving 60 days' written notice to the Licensor.

4.3 Upon termination, the Licensee shall vacate the Premises within 30 days of the notice period.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT

5.1 The Licensee has deposited with the Licensor a sum of Rs. 1,10,000/- (Rupees One Lakh Ten Thousand Only) as Security Deposit, equivalent to 4 (Four) months' Licence Fee.

5.2 The Licensor may deduct from the Security Deposit any amounts due on account of damage to the Premises, unpaid Licence Fee, unpaid utility charges, or any other breach of this Agreement.

5.3 The balance of the Security Deposit, after all deductions, shall be refunded to the Licensee within 30 days of vacating the Premises. No interest shall be payable on the Security Deposit.

6. USE OF PREMISES

6.1 The Licensee shall use the Premises solely for residential purposes.

6.2 The Licensee shall not make any structural alterations without prior written consent.

6.3 The Licensee shall not sublet or assign the Premises.

[[page:4]]

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

7.1 The Licensee shall maintain the Premises in good condition and shall be responsible for all minor repairs up to Rs. 5,000/- per instance.

7.2 Major repairs and structural maintenance shall be the responsibility of the Licensor.

7.3 The Licensor shall attend to reported maintenance issues within 14 days of receiving written notice from the Licensee. Emergency repairs affecting habitability shall be addressed within 48 hours.

8. UTILITIES

8.1 The Licensee shall pay all utility charges directly to the service providers.

8.2 The maintenance charges for the apartment complex shall be borne by the Licensee.

9. INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 The Licensee shall indemnify and hold harmless the Licensor against all claims arising from the Licensee's use of the Premises or breach of this Agreement.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

10.2 Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be referred to mediation. If mediation fails within 30 days, the dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed mutually by the Parties, in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat of arbitration shall be Bangalore, Karnataka.

[[page:5]]

11. GENERAL PROVISIONS

11.1 Any amendment to this Agreement shall be in writing and signed by both Parties.

11.2 Any waiver by the Licensor of any breach shall not constitute a waiver of any subsequent breach.

11.3 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

12. NOTICE

12.1 Any notice shall be given in writing and delivered personally or sent by registered post or email.
